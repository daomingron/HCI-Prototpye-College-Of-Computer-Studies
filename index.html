Excellence in Computing

Nurturing Competent, Ethical, and Globally Competitive IT Professionals

The College of Computer Studies at De La Salle John Bosco College is dedicated to producing innovative technology professionals who harness the transformative power of computing to solve real-world challenges in industry, governance, and society.

Faith

Integrating Christian values and high ethical standards into technological practice.

Service

Communion

Academic Curriculum

Undergraduate Degree Programs

Our curricula are compliant with Commission on Higher Education (CHED) standards and updated regularly to align with international computing industry frameworks.

4-Year Degree • CHED Recognized

BS in Information Technology

BSIT Program

Focuses on the utilization, management, and deployment of computing technologies, database architecture, network infrastructure, web systems, and IT security.

Specialization Tracks:
Cybersecurity Network Administration Web & Mobile Development Database Systems
8 Semesters + Practicum
4-Year Degree • CHED Recognized

BS in Computer Science

BSCS Program

Emphasizes computational theory, mathematical algorithms, software engineering principles, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and systems programming.

Specialization Tracks:
Artificial Intelligence Software Engineering Data Science & Analytics Game Development
8 Semesters + Research Thesis
